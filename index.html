06/FAQ

Questions? Answered.

Everything you need to know before getting started.

What is the free sample?+

Before you pay anything, I create a short preview of your project — a 5-10 second clip, a mockup, or a draft. This way you can see the quality and style first, and decide if you want to move forward. No risk at all.

How long does delivery take?+

Most small projects (AI videos, photo-to-motion, UGC ads) are delivered in 24-48 hours. Larger projects like websites or full app designs may take 3-7 days depending on complexity. Rush delivery is available too!

What do I need to provide?+

Just share your idea! For AI videos: a script or voice recording. For anime/motion: your photos or footage. For websites/apps: your content, branding preferences, and reference links. I guide you through the rest.

Do you offer revisions?+

Absolutely! Every order includes at least 2 rounds of revisions. I want you to be 100% happy with the result. Most clients love the first draft, but changes are always welcome.

What payment methods do you accept?+

On Fiverr, all standard payment methods are supported (cards, PayPal, etc.). For direct orders, I accept PayPal, bank transfer, and UPI. Payment is split: 50% upfront, 50% on approval.

Can I use the videos for commercial purposes?+

Yes! You get full commercial rights to all delivered work. Use it on your website, social media, ads, presentations — anywhere. The work is yours to keep and use forever.

How do I get started?+

Simply click "Hire on Fiverr" or message me on WhatsApp. Share your idea, and I'll send a free sample. Once you approve, we move to production. It's that easy!
